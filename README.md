# 🏥 HealthPilot AI

### AI-Powered Personal Health Copilot

**Transforming fragmented healthcare information into meaningful, actionable insights.**

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://aipoweredpersonalhealthcopilot.netlify.app/

---

## 💡 Problem Statement

Healthcare information is often scattered across prescriptions, diagnostic reports, medical records, and medication schedules. Patients may struggle to understand medical terminology, track changes in test results, organize their medications, and maintain a clear overview of their healthcare journey.

This fragmentation makes it difficult for individuals to stay informed and organized.

## 🚀 Our Solution

**HealthPilot AI** is an AI-powered personal healthcare companion that brings essential health management tools together in one platform.

It helps users organize medical information, understand diagnostic reports, track medications, monitor health trends, and interact with an AI health copilot for accessible explanations of healthcare information.

Our goal is to make personal healthcare information easier to understand, navigate, and manage.

## 🧠 How It Works

1. **Access your health workspace:** Sign in and explore the healthcare dashboard.
2. **Organize health information:** Use the medical records, reports, and medication management features.
3. **Understand your information:** Explore available report analysis tools and ask the AI copilot general healthcare questions.
4. **Stay organized:** Manage reminders and personal health goals.
5. **Review your health journey:** Use the available timeline, comparisons, and visual insights to review recorded information.

*The functionality available depends on the features implemented and the data provided by the user.*

## 🔒 Privacy & Medical Safety

HealthPilot AI is designed as a healthcare information management and assistance platform.

* AI-generated responses may be inaccurate and should not be treated as a medical diagnosis.
* Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals before making medical decisions.
* The platform is not intended for medical emergencies.
* Users should understand the applicable data handling and privacy protections before uploading sensitive medical information.
* The simulated ABHA integration does not connect to production government health systems.
